# Strategy v3: five rule sets, every signal traded

Built on 2026-11-13. It replaces everything before it: the old five indicator strategies, their v2 and news variants, the aggressive playbook accounts, the confluence gate, cooldowns, lessons, probation and the retired list.

## Why

The bots had taken almost no trades for a week. Between a signal and an order sat a stack of filters, and together they let nearly nothing through:

- a hard-coded retired list (every 5m, 15m and 30m setup, plus five 1h and 4h setups),
- probation (shadow-only until replays proved an edge),
- a confluence score that had to reach 65 of 100,
- lessons, cooldowns after losses, a news gate,
- on the aggressive accounts, a playbook that stopped trading 15m altogether.

v3 removes all of it. A rule set gives a signal, the signal is traded. The plan is to run five rule sets for 2 to 4 weeks, read the results per rule set, drop the weak ones and improve the strong ones.

## What trades

Five rule sets × three timeframes (15m, 1h, 4h) × BTC, ETH, SOL: **15 accounts of $100**, one position per coin per account.

| Rule set | Idea | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| Fair Value Gap | First retrace into a displacement gap, with the 200 EMA | [fvg.md](fvg.md) |
| Order Block | First retest of the last opposite candle before a break of structure | [order_block.md](order_block.md) |
| Bollinger + RSI Reversion | Fade a close outside the band at an RSI extreme | [bb_rsi_reversion.md](bb_rsi_reversion.md) |
| EMA Pullback | First dip to the 20 EMA in a 20/50 EMA trend | [ema_pullback.md](ema_pullback.md) |
| Donchian Breakout | 20-bar breakout on volume, trailing the 10-bar channel | [donchian_breakout.md](donchian_breakout.md) |

Fair Value Gap and Order Block trade by choice. The other three were picked from the candidates below by their result on a year of history.

## How the five were chosen

There is no record of other traders' results to rank strategies by, so the ranking is a backtest: ten rule sets in common use (ICT concepts, price action, support and resistance, patterns, and the two best of the old set), each replayed on a year of Binance candles with the same code the live bots run.

Run it yourself (offline, from the candle cache): `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/experiments/v3-select.ts`. The full output is in [`scripts/experiments/v3-select.out.txt`](../../scripts/experiments/v3-select.out.txt).

Setup: 365 days to 2026-10-29, every signal traded, taker fee 0.02%, **0.05% slippage on every taker fill**. "Paper avg R" is the same run without slippage. The paper accounts charge fees but fill at the quoted price, so that column is what the dashboard will tend to show.

## What the backtest says, plainly

**Every rule set loses money over the year once realistic costs are charged.** Not one of the ten has a positive average R at 0.05% slippage across its three timeframes.

- **The entries are about a coin flip.** Win rates sit near 33% with a 2R target, which is breakeven before costs. The losses are the costs.
- **15m is where the money goes.** Stops there are close to the 0.4% minimum, so fees and slippage take about 0.15R to 0.3R from every trade. In the backtest 12 of the 15 live setups' 15m accounts and all but one of the others end the year busted or near it. On paper (no slippage) the same accounts hover around breakeven.
- **4h is the only place with positive numbers**, and the samples are small: Bollinger + RSI 4h +0.124R over 48 trades, Donchian 4h +0.090R over 201, Double Top 4h +0.046R over 77. Donchian 1h is flat (+0.008R over 759). None of these is proof of an edge.
- **Nothing was tuned to look better.** One pass over target size, stop buffer, trend filter and displacement size found no setting that helped in both halves of the year, so the defaults stayed. Tuning further on this data would only fit the past.

So the 2 to 4 week trial is an experiment, not an income. Expect about 22 trades a day across the 15 accounts, most of them on 15m, and expect the paper results to look better than a real account would, because paper fills have no slippage.

## Reading the trial

**Bots → AI analysis → Rule sets** shows each rule set over all its timeframes: trades, win rate, average R, profit factor, net. The per-setup table below it splits by timeframe and adds the slippage-adjusted numbers (`Avg R (slip)`).

Suggested rules for the review:

- Judge nothing on fewer than 30 trades. On 15m that takes days; on 4h it takes the whole trial or longer.
- Read `Avg R (slip)`, not the raw return. A rule set that only wins without slippage does not win.
- Drop a rule set (or one of its timeframes) when it has 30+ trades and its average R after slippage is below zero.
- Keep and improve what stays positive after slippage. The first candidates to cut, from the backtest, are the 15m accounts.

To drop a rule set, remove it from `STRATEGIES` in `supabase/functions/_shared/bot/strategies.ts` and redeploy `bot-tick`. To drop a timeframe for one rule set, give it a `timeframes` list. Its account stops trading; the history stays.

## What changed in the code

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| `structure.ts` (new) | Swing points, breaks of structure, fair value gaps, order blocks, support/resistance levels, pin bars and engulfing candles. Closed bars only, no look-ahead. |
| `strategies.ts` | Ten candidate rule sets (`CANDIDATES`), five live (`STRATEGIES`). |
| `tick.ts` | A signal is opened unless the account is busted, already holds that coin, the price is already past the stop or target, or the account cannot carry the order. Each of those is logged. Nothing else stands between a signal and an order. |
| `risk.ts` | `planEntry`: a stop tighter than 0.4% of price is widened to 0.4% and the target moved out by the same factor, where it used to be dropped. |
| `backtest.ts` | Same entry planning as live; no gate or discipline options. |
| `analyze.ts`, `analysis.ts` | Reviews, statistics, rankings and reports only. No lessons, no tuning, no replays. The analyzer never changes what is traded. |
| Removed | `confluence.ts`, `discipline.ts`, `tuning.ts`, `news.ts`, `aggro.ts`, `playbook.ts`, `aggro-seed.ts`, the `bot-news` function and its cron job. |
| Database | `bot.sql` drops the old functions; `bot_reset.sql` deletes the old accounts, trades and learning tables. |
| Tests | Detector and rule fixtures drawn by hand (`bot-structure.test.ts`), entry planning, and an 8-day simulated run that checks every signal against its trade in both directions (`bot-db.test.ts`). |
